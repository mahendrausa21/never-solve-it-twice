# ShopEasy Support Knowledge Base

Fictional e-commerce company used for the "Never Solve It Twice" hackathon demo.
Each entry has a problem, a verified fix, and an escalation rule.

---

## KB-001: Refund not received
**Problem:** Customer says the refund has not arrived after 7 or more days.
**Fix:** Check the payment status in the billing dashboard. If the refund shows "processed", ask the customer to wait 3 more bank working days. If it shows "pending" or "failed", resend the refund.
**Escalate to:** Billing team if the refund is still missing after 10 days.

## KB-002: Order not delivered
**Problem:** Order shows "delivered" but the customer did not receive it.
**Fix:** Ask the customer to check with neighbours and the building reception, then wait 24 hours. If still missing, open a carrier investigation.
**Escalate to:** Logistics team after the carrier investigation is opened.

## KB-003: Wrong item received
**Problem:** Customer received a different product than ordered.
**Fix:** Apologise, ask for a photo of the item and the packing label, then create a free return pickup and ship the correct item.
**Escalate to:** Not needed unless the correct item is out of stock; then offer a full refund.

## KB-004: Password reset email not arriving
**Problem:** Customer does not receive the password reset email.
**Fix:** Ask them to check the spam folder, confirm the email address on the account, and request the reset again after 5 minutes.
**Escalate to:** Account security team if the email address on file is wrong.

## KB-005: Payment deducted but order not placed
**Problem:** Money was taken from the account but no order confirmation was received.
**Fix:** Search for the transaction ID. If no order exists, the amount is auto-reversed within 5 to 7 working days. Share the transaction ID with the customer.
**Escalate to:** Billing team if the amount is not reversed after 7 working days.

## KB-006: Coupon code not working
**Problem:** Discount coupon is rejected at checkout.
**Fix:** Check expiry date, minimum order value, and whether the coupon is limited to certain categories. If valid but still failing, apply the discount manually.
**Escalate to:** Marketing team if the coupon is valid and the system still rejects it.

## KB-007: Order cancellation request
**Problem:** Customer wants to cancel an order.
**Fix:** If the order has not shipped, cancel it and refund within 5 to 7 working days. If it has shipped, refuse cancellation and offer a return after delivery.
**Escalate to:** Not needed.

## KB-008: Damaged product on arrival
**Problem:** Product arrived broken or damaged.
**Fix:** Ask for photos of the product and the outer box. Offer a free replacement or a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.
**Escalate to:** Quality team if the same product is reported damaged 3 or more times in a week.

## KB-009: Cannot log in to account
**Problem:** Customer cannot log in even with the correct password.
**Fix:** Ask them to clear browser cache, try another browser, and check that Caps Lock is off. If still failing, unlock the account from the admin panel.
**Escalate to:** Account security team if there are signs of suspicious login attempts.

## KB-010: Delivery delayed beyond promised date
**Problem:** Order has not arrived by the promised delivery date.
**Fix:** Check the tracking status. If delayed by the carrier, apologise and give a new estimated date. If delayed by more than 5 days, offer a cancellation with full refund.
**Escalate to:** Logistics team if tracking has not updated for 3 days.

## KB-011: Invoice or GST bill request
**Problem:** Customer needs an invoice or tax bill for an order.
**Fix:** Send the invoice from the order details page. If the customer needs a business GST invoice, collect the GST number and regenerate the invoice.
**Escalate to:** Finance team for invoices older than 90 days.

## KB-012: Subscription charged after cancellation
**Problem:** Customer was charged for a subscription they had cancelled.
**Fix:** Confirm the cancellation date in the account history. If cancelled before the billing date, refund the charge in full.
**Escalate to:** Billing team if the cancellation date is unclear.

## KB-013: Product out of stock after ordering
**Problem:** Customer is told the item they ordered is out of stock.
**Fix:** Offer a full refund or a similar product at the same price. Provide an expected restock date if known.
**Escalate to:** Not needed.

## KB-014: Return pickup not happening
**Problem:** A return was approved but no one came to collect the item.
**Fix:** Reschedule the pickup for the next working day and confirm the customer's address and phone number. Offer a drop-off point if pickup fails twice.
**Escalate to:** Logistics team after 2 failed pickup attempts.

## KB-015: Address change after ordering
**Problem:** Customer wants to change the delivery address.
**Fix:** If the order has not shipped, update the address. If it has shipped, contact the carrier to redirect, and tell the customer it is not guaranteed.
**Escalate to:** Logistics team for redirect requests.
